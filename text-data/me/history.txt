  <p>I was born in Sonora, California. I don't really remember that place, as the family left when I was too small to have memories. My first memories were from a place called TwentyNine Palms, CA. From there, I remember:
  <ul>
    <li>Catching lizards</li>
    <li>Hitting large cacti with sticks</li>
    <li>Exploring the desert</li>
    <li>Coloring a Mickey Mouse face in Kindergarten, but forgot that he had a mouse nose and drew a human nose instead</li>
    <li>A Native American presentation, including dance, in Kindergarten</li>
    <li>Sidewinder tracks and snake skins</li>
    <li>My brothers: Leif and Daniel</li>
    <li>Drawing pictures in homemade books</li>
    <li>Visiting my great grandfather once</li>
    <li>My older brother's friend, Scott, and his Star Wars toys</li>
  </ul>
  </p>
  <p>We left California when I was still in Kindergarten and moved to Merriman, Nebraska (population likely measured in negative numbers). From there, I still remember many of the names of the kids with whom I went to class. I remember cottonwood lake, where walleye could be caught and periodically going to Valentine when we needed stuff. I also remember my little brother's class had only three students.</p>
  <p>I lived in Chappell, NE from third grade to seventh. Long story short, the number of schools I had attended prior to secondary education was eight. My parents were free-spirited individuals who apparently didn't really feel connected to a single place.</p>
  <p>The trend continued after gaining my freedom, as I really did feel like there had to be more to this country than what Nebraska offered. The first state to which I had moved was Phoenix, AZ. Afterwards, there was Ohio, before finally coming to Colorado, where my secondary education began.</p>
  <p>I knew before I started that I wanted to be in the Computer Science major. At that time, I didn't know that it was eventually going to be the boilerplate degree that everybody wants to attempt (because lul $). I loved computers and the idea of writing my own programs. It did not disappoint, though as many might expect, the journey itself was pretty hectic. The problem was that I wasn't exactly the greatest student in high school, so Northeastern Junior College was step one.</p>
  <p>The degree that I had obtained was in general sciences. The instructors there were amazing! Mr. Vair is the best math instructor one can hope for and everybody else there made it really a fantastic experience. Unfortunately, that school did not offer a computer science degree, so once that was finished, I applied -- and was accepted -- to Colorado State University.</p>
  <p>It was the most difficult experience of my life, but I am grateful for the knowledge obtained there. The field itself really is as exciting as I had imagined. My first post-education employment opportunity was with Deloitte Digital, where I remain to this day. I met my would-be wife soon afterward.</p>
  <p>That brings me to today, 5/16/2019, when I decided to add some content to this website that I am building. I have been at it for almost two weeks and have many plans for it, including possibly even a fish bowl game.</p>

  <p>Of course, as this is only a few paragraphs, you might well imagine that a few details were left-out. They were! My existence has really been pock-marked with whirlwinds of excitement and there really are some interesting details. It may not have always felt this way, but I really am happy that I did take the path seldom-traveled.</p>
